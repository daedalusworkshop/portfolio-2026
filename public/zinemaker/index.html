How it works

  1. Start with anything Give your agent an idea, story, image, link, or file.
  2. It makes the zine Your agent uses GPT Image 2 to create an eight-page zine laid out on one sheet of paper.
  3. It prints it Your agent sends the finished sheet to a printer on your Wi-Fi. Fold it into a zine.
